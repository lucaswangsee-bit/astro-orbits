    🛰 Ephemeris · RA / Dec

    Where a body appears in Earth's sky, from real orbital elements — the same coordinates you measure to determine an orbit.

    📐 Orbit Determination Lab

    Three sky positions are enough to recover a whole orbit: 3 observations × (RA, Dec) = 6 measured numbers = the 6 orbital elements. This is the inverse of the Ephemeris panel — there a known orbit predicts a position; here measured positions solve for the orbit.

    ❔ How to use Cosmic Orbits

    Everything you need to find your way around the 3D view

    Navigation

    • Drag to rotate the view around the centre
    • Scroll (or pinch) to zoom in and out
    • Click any body — or any chip in the control panel — to open its data, live physics and full derivation

    Colour legend

    • Stars — the Sun, and every star in the Nearby Stars map
    • Planets & moons — the eight planets and the Moon
    • Comets (italic labels) — icy bodies with Sun-facing tails
    • Asteroids — real minor planets solved from JPL elements
    • Distance rings — light-year reference circles in the star map

    Keyboard shortcuts

    SpacePlay / pause the simulation
    RReset the view to its default framing
    OShow / hide the orbit lines
    ?Open this help panel
    EscClose any open panel

    Labels fade in and out as you move: when two would overlap on screen, the less important one is hidden automatically — nothing is missing, just zoom in and it comes back.
